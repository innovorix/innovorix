# 🌐 Innovorix — Smart Institute Management Platform

Innovorix is an **AI-driven Institute Management System** built to simplify and automate the operations of schools, colleges, and academies.  
We empower educational institutions with modern technology — enabling smarter decisions, faster workflows, and connected learning experiences.

---

## 🚀 Our Vision  
To transform traditional institute management into a **fully digital, data-driven ecosystem** — helping schools and universities operate with efficiency, transparency, and intelligence.

---

## 🧠 What We Offer

### 🎓 Education ERP Platform  
A robust, role-based web and mobile system for:
- Student, Guardian, and Staff Portals  
- Fee & Attendance Management  
- Academic Timetable and Results  
- Real-time Analytics and Reports  

### 💬 Smart Communication  
Integrated with **Meta WhatsApp Cloud API** to send instant updates, announcements, and alerts directly to guardians and staff.

### 📱 Mobile App (Capacitor + Next.js)  
A seamless mobile experience for students and staff with real-time push notifications, attendance tracking, and performance summaries.

### ☁️ Cloud Infrastructure & Automation  
Powered by **Supabase**, **Firebase**, and **Oracle Cloud**, Innovorix delivers reliable performance with auto-scaling, logging, and monitoring.

---

## 🧩 Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** Next.js, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript  
- **Backend:** Supabase (PostgreSQL), Firebase  
- **Mobile:** Capacitor.js  
- **APIs:** Meta WhatsApp Cloud, Cloudflare, Google Analytics  
- **DevOps:** Docker, Oracle Cloud, systemd
